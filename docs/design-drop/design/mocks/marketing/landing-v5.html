On the slow death of the workspace tab

For most of the last decade, the workspace has been a tab — one of many — inside a browser-shaped vessel called The App. The tab pretended to be a window, the window pretended to be a desktop, the desktop pretended not to exist.

That pretending is finally collapsing. What the OS does well — focus, switching, identity — wins over what The App tried to recreate.
