system & utility

The structural plumbing of the library. tab-panel and error-boundary have a real visual surface — shown here Existing vs New (toggle top-left). portal and toast-host are behavior-only — they teleport or bridge but render no chrome of their own, so they're documented as utility cards rather than restyled. All four are ground truth in source; the design work is the surface where one exists. Switch themes right.

sui-portal

behavior-only

No chrome of its own. It pulls its light-DOM children out and appends them into a target element elsewhere in the document — so overlays escape overflow:hidden clips and stacking contexts. Retargets live if target changes; cleans up on disconnect.

sui-portal

Teleport light DOM to a target. Default target is body.

sui-toast-host

behavior-only

A zero-chrome bridge. It listens for ship:notify events on the nearest sui-shell/ship-shell (or document.body) and forwards them to a toast container's show(). The visible toast is sui-toast — the host just wires the event to it.
