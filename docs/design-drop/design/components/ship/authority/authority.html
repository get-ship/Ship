authority · pack

Authority made legible. Every effect an agent causes passed a gate — this pack makes the gate, the grants, and the record first-class. The checkpoint (approval), the queue that batches it, the grants (capability-bar · policy-matrix · principal-list), the live verdict log, and the two shared atoms (blast-tier · gate-decision) that read identically everywhere. All compiled live from .sui, tokens only. Switch themes at right.
