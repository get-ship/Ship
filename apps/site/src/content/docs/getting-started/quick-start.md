Ship is in **private beta** — there's no public installer yet. [Join the waitlist](/waitlist/) and you'll get access as the beta opens up.

## How install will work

When the public beta lands, getting Ship is a normal app install — no terminal required:

- a **GUI installer** you download and double-click, or
- your **package manager** (`brew`, `apt`, `dnf`, `winget`).

Either way you get the same two pieces — the `ship` CLI and the `shipd` daemon — registered to start automatically, with setup opening in your browser on first run.

## First launch

The first time you open Ship, it launches in your browser and walks you through three things:

1. **Welcome** — orients you. Ship runs entirely on your machine.
2. **Pick a folder** — start a new space or open an existing one; Ship sets up the workspace.
3. **Connect a provider** — Claude, Cursor, Gemini, Codex, or OpenCode. Paste an API key, or skip and connect later.

Then you land on the dashboard. From there, [add your first agent →](/docs/your-first-agent/)
