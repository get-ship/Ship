By installing this software you acknowledge that:

  * The installer is not code-signed for v1. Your OS may show a
    security warning (Gatekeeper on macOS, SmartScreen on Windows);
    open the installer anyway to proceed. Code-signed builds are
    tracked in the project roadmap.

  * Ship writes configuration and state to ~/.ship/ and registers a
    background daemon (`shipd`) as a user-level service (systemd
    --user on Linux, launchd user agent on macOS, startup task on
    Windows). Uninstall removes the service and ~/.ship/.

  * No telemetry is collected beyond the local event log in
    ~/.ship/platform.db.
